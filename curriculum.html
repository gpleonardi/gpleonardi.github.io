  • June 2020–today. Full Professor at the Department of Mathematics of the University of Trento.

  • 1989–1994. Degree in Mathematics at the University of Modena.

Research interests

Main research areas: Calculus of Variations, Geometric Measure Theory, Machine Learning

Current topics:

  • Isoperimetric problems for sets and clusters, quantitative stability of minimizers, and capillarity in the presence of singular forces and non-smooth boundaries.

  • Crystallization, Wulff shapes, and fluctuation estimates in periodic lattices.

  • Mathematical aspects of Deep Neural Networks.

University of Trento (synthesis)

From 1997 to 2000 I have been lecturer for the courses of Calculus I and Calculus II, respectively at the Faculty of Economics and at the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Trento.

In the academic year 1999–2000, I taught a course entitled “Analisi II con Maple” at the Faculty of Engineering in Trento. The course was aimed at presenting some essential Calculus II topics in the form of a laboratory using the software Maple. A subsequent online version of the course has been made available starting from the academic year 2000–2001 (see web page http://gpleo.altervista.org/corsi/corso_maple/corso.html).
