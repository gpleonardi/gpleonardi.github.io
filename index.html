About

A brief biography

I have been Full Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Trento since June 2020, following an appointment as Associate Professor from 2018. Previously, I held Assistant Professor positions at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia (2005–2018) and the University of Padova (2001–2005). I obtained my PhD in Mathematics at the University of Trento in 1998, after graduating in Mathematics at the University of Modena.

Research

Main interests

01

Variational problems
& geometric analysis

Isoperimetric problems for sets and clusters; the Selection Principle and the quantitative stability of minimizers; PMC equations with singular forces and non-smooth boundaries.

02

Theory of discrete varifolds

Approximate curvatures and their convergence properties, with applications to geometric flows for point-clouds.

03

Crystallization
& discrete geometry

Asymptotic Wulff shapes and fluctuation estimates in periodic lattices.

04

Mathematics
of Machine Learning

Mathematical aspects of deep neural networks, including quantization and measures of model complexity.
